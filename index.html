How much does this cost?
We build a custom quote for you depending on which services you'd like to add. We can handle it end to end, but we respect that you may only want to start with a few services first.
Will the AI agents sound like a robot?
No. Voice agents use the latest natural voice models, most customers don't realise they're not talking to a person. You also pick the voice. Want it to sound like you? We can clone yours.
How is this different from a marketing agency?
Three real differences. First, we work only with florists, so we don't bill you to learn what peonies are or how peak season works. Second, we're operators with experience running ads, outreach, and growth across multiple businesses, not a generic marketing shop. And third, we integrate AI into how we work for speed and efficiency, which is why one team can deliver what would otherwise mean hiring multiple people in-house or juggling five different agencies.
What if I already have someone doing my social?
No problem at all. We slot in wherever you need us, whether that's everything except social, or just one or two things. The shape is yours to define.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. You can cancel on one month's notice so we can wrap things up properly and do a smooth handover.
